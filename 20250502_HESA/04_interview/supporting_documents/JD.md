## Introduction 
 
The  University  of  Manchester  is  seeking  to  develop  its  capacity  and  capability  to  undertake 
detailed  analysis  across  a  range  of  data  sets,  to  enable  the  generation  of  the  insight  that  can 
inform  decision  making  and  action  planning.  The  post  holder  will  become  a  member  of  the 
University’s analytical community and will have a  key  role  in  helping  us  transform  our  ways  of 
working and outputs. The role sits within the Planning Directorate which supports the University’s 
strategic planning processes, provides key management information and insight to senior decision 
makers and manages the submission of statutory student returns to the regulator.  
 
## Overall Purpose of the Job 
 
This  post  will  undertake analysis  on  a  range  of  internal  and  external  benchmarking  datasets to 
generate  insight  that  supports  decision-making,  aligned  to  the  University  goals.  The  post  will 
support the Lead Data Analyst in improving the visualisation, reporting and dissemination of key 
insights for  different  audiences,  alongside the  promotion  of  a  self-service automation model  for 
routine requests.  
 

## Key Responsibilities, Accountabilities or Duties 
 
- Independently produce and regularly update data dashboards to ensure accurate, timely, and 
easily understandable information to support strategic decision-making across the institution 
- Attain expertise in key data sets e.g. HESA student, staff and finance data, NSS, Graduate 
Outcomes,  OfS  Outcomes  and  Experience  metrics,  differential  Student  Attainment  and  the 
mechanics of League Tables, and to act as a key contact for colleagues across the university 
- Continuously review and improve data presentation and availability to ensure the outputs of 
dashboards  are  aligned  with  strategic  priorities  and  to  enable  extraction  of  maximum  value 
from key data sources 
- Collaborate closely with the Data Architect and Data Modeller to understand the design and 
functionality of dimensional data models and to ensure data flows are current and data models 
remain fit for purpose 
- To produce summary reports and key analysis relevant to University Executive and 
Committees across the University both at institution level and within the context of the wider 
sector 
- To  undertake  bespoke  analyses  in  response  to  queries  from  the  University  Executive  and 
senior managers, or to follow up questions from own analyses 
- Act a central point of contact for stakeholders and analysts across the institution to ensure that 
data analysis undertaken across the institution is consistent and co-ordinated 
- Proactively  engage  with  colleagues,  staff  networks  and  committees  to  build  constructive 
relationships 
- Capture requirements from colleagues, and understand what questions need to be answered 
from the data 
- Implement procedures to keep data accurate and up to date, and to work proactively to remedy 
data quality issues 
- Maintain confidentiality of information in line with data protection requirements and University 
policy and to operate in line with GDPR and best practice guidance in data security and quality 
- To  undertake  such  other  duties  as  may  be  required  by the  Lead  Data  Analyst  or  Head  of 
Business Intelligence commensurate with the level of responsibility of the role

## 职位简介

曼彻斯特大学希望提升在多种数据集上的分析能力，以在决策和行动计划中产生洞察力。该岗位将加入大学分析团队，在转变工作方式和成果方面发挥关键作用。职位隶属于规划司，通过支持大学战略规划流程、为高级决策者提供关键管理信息与洞察，并向监管机构提交法定学生数据，支撑大学运作。

## 职位总体目标

该岗位负责对多种内部及外部的基准数据集开展分析，以产生符合大学目标的决策支持洞察。岗位需支持首席数据分析师提升不同受众的可视化、报告和洞察发布方式，并推动日常需求的自助自动化模式。

## 主要职责、责任或任务

- 独立制作并定期更新数据仪表板，确保为全校战略决策提供准确、及时且易于理解的信息
- 熟悉核心数据集，例如 HESA 学生、教职员工和财务数据、全国学生调查（NSS）、毕业生结果（Graduate Outcomes）、学生事务办公室（OfS）成果与体验指标、学生成绩差异及各类排行榜机制，并为全校同事提供咨询
- 持续审查和改进数据呈现与可获取性，确保仪表板输出符合战略重点，并最大化关键数据源的价值
- 与数据架构师和数据建模师紧密合作，了解维度数据模型的设计与功能，确保数据流更新且模型仍适用
- 为大学执行委员会及全校各委员会撰写机构层面与更广泛行业背景的摘要报告和关键分析
- 针对大学执行委员会和高级管理层的需求，开展定制分析或跟进自身分析中提出的问题
- 作为全校利益相关者和分析师的中心联络点，确保数据分析在全校范围内保持一致与协调
- 积极与同事、员工网络及委员会沟通，建立建设性关系
- 收集同事需求，明确数据需回答的问题
- 建立流程以保持数据准确、及时，并主动解决数据质量问题
- 依据数据保护要求和大学政策维护信息机密，遵守 GDPR 与数据安全及质量最佳实践
- 执行首席数据分析师或商业智能负责人交办的其他职责，确保责任水平匹配
